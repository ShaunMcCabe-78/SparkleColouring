Sparkle Colouring

Pick a picture, then match the numbers to the paint pots.

For grown-ups: choose a colour, then colour inside any shape with the same number. The crayon stays inside the lines, so scribbles are fine. Once a shape is nearly all coloured it fills in by itself. The magic wand fills a whole shape with one tap. Two fingers zoom in. Pictures are saved on this iPad as she goes.

Your own pictures: tap "Add a picture" and choose a colouring page saved in Photos or Files. Simple drawings with bold black lines and closed shapes work best. These have no numbers, so she can pick any colour for any shape.
